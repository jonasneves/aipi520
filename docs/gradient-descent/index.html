What does the sigmoid do?

It turns the score into a probability between 0 and 1. That's the S-curve over the tumors: small radius, near 0; large radius, near 1.

What is gradient descent?

There's no formula that jumps to the best w and b, so we start somewhere, measure the slope of the cost, and step against it. Repeat until the slope is about 0.

What is it doing each step?

Every tumor reports its error × input. The model averages those pulls and moves w and b a step against them. Badly wrong calls pull hardest; right ones barely pull.

Data: Wisconsin Diagnostic Breast Cancer dataset (569 biopsies), as shipped with scikit-learn. Feature: mean radius of the cell nuclei. Scaling uses the mean 14.13 and standard deviation 3.52 of all 569. Accuracy figures are on the same 569 biopsies the model learned from, so they show how well it fits, not how it would do on new patients. The six tumors are dataset rows 425, 84, 8, 227, 0 and 302. Model: p = σ(w·x̃ + b), trained by batch gradient descent on the average log loss, starting from w = 0, b = 0.
